# Pricing policy

- Use only the subject's permitted price grid, including unchanged price. No price increases.
- Require `eligibility='ready'`, positive stock, unexpired goods, supported model and complete exposure history.
- Require a completed sales boundary with delivery age at most 120 seconds. Recheck after a delay.
- Compare every permitted option at the same store, SKU, cutoff, stock and model.
- Expected receipts = price × expected units. Expected waste = stock − expected units.
- Net recovery = receipts − selling cost × units − disposal cost × waste.
- Contribution = net recovery − purchase cost × starting stock. Stock is already purchased.
- Incremental profit = option contribution − unchanged-price contribution.
- Prefer highest modeled recovery. If tied, keep the current price; never present a tie as certain uplift.
- The demand scenarios are sensitivity assumptions, not statistical confidence intervals.
- Recommend; the learner reviews and approves in the workbench. Changed source evidence requires a new comparison.
- Report an applied price only after an acknowledged receipt. Report observed performance only from that receipt's event series.
- Remaining stock becomes waste at expiry; before expiry call it remaining stock.
- Explain the synthetic comparison and its shared random demand conditions. It is not proof of causal uplift in real stores.
